Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you come to my address?

Yes. We are a mobile service for Smederevo and surrounding areas — we bring all professional equipment to you.

Is ozone treatment safe?

Yes. It is carried out in an empty, unoccupied vehicle. After treatment and ventilation, the interior is safe to use.

Can stains really be completely removed?

Extraction equipment pulls dirt from deep within fabric. Results depend on the stain and material, so we assess the condition honestly before starting.

Will moisture remain after cleaning?

Professional extraction removes almost all moisture during cleaning, so surfaces are usable much sooner than after traditional manual washing.

How can I book an appointment?

The easiest way is to message us on Instagram. We'll arrange the date, time and details directly.

Do you work with all types of vehicles and furniture?

Yes — from city cars to premium models, as well as sofas, armchairs, mattresses and other upholstered items.
